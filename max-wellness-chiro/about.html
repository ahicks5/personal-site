Meet Your Doctors

A husband-and-wife team dedicated to your health.

"We listen — to understand your lifestyle and motivation for seeking relief — then deliver care so effectively and efficiently that you get real results without unnecessary cost or delay."

Dr. William "Tony" Maxwell

DC, ART — Co-Owner

Before entering the world of chiropractic, Dr. Tony was a 2000 4A Texas State Champion football player for the Black Cats. He went on to play college ball and later worked in the petroleum industry before following his true calling to help people heal through chiropractic care.

He earned his education at Wharton County Junior College before attending Texas Chiropractic College, where he completed a Bachelor of Science in Human Biology and his Doctor of Chiropractic degree.

Dr. Tony is certified in Active Release Technique (ART), Athletic TIPS, and post-concussion return-to-play protocols, giving him a specialized edge in treating athletes and active individuals.

He currently serves as Adjunct Faculty-Preceptor at Texas Chiropractic College, mentoring the next generation of chiropractors.

His commitment to the community has earned him notable recognition, including the 2019 Texas Regional Bank Hometown Hero award and the 2021 VFW Patriot of the Year award.

Outside the office, Dr. Tony is a proud father of four daughters.

LM

Dr. Leslie Catherine Maxwell

DC — Co-Owner, "The Dancing Doc"

Dr. Leslie's path to chiropractic began in the world of dance. She attended Kilgore College as a Rangerette and Swingster, developing the discipline and body awareness that would later shape her approach to patient care.

She earned a Bachelor of Arts in Theatre and Dance and a Bachelor of Science in Kinesiology from the University of Texas at Austin.

An acute hip injury during college led her to discover the healing potential of chiropractic care firsthand, inspiring a career change that merged her understanding of movement with the science of spinal health.

Dr. Leslie graduated from Texas Chiropractic College with a Bachelor of Science in Human Biology and her Doctor of Chiropractic degree.

She currently serves as Adjunct Lecturer at UT Austin's Department of Theatre and Dance, and as Ballet Mistress at Studio W Dance Collective, maintaining deep ties to the performing arts community.

Her background gives her a unique understanding of movement, performance, and the body — making her especially effective with dancers, athletes, and anyone looking to move better and feel better.

Together with Dr. Tony, she is a proud mother of four daughters.
