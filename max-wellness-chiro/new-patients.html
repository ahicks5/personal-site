Getting Started

Your First Visit

We make getting started simple. Here is what to expect at each step of the process.

Complete Intake Forms

Complete your patient paperwork online before arriving via our secure patient portal. This saves time and lets the doctor review your information ahead of your appointment.

Consultation & Exam

The doctor will perform a thorough consultation, discuss your health history, lifestyle, and goals. A comprehensive examination helps us identify the root cause of your concerns.

Your First Treatment

If appropriate, you will receive your first adjustment during this visit. Please plan for 30–45 minutes for your initial appointment so we can give you the attention you deserve.

Billing

Insurance & Payment

We accept most major insurance plans and offer flexible payment options to make chiropractic care accessible.

Accepted Insurance

  • Most major medical PPO plans
  • Most major medical HMO plans
  • Visa, Mastercard, Amex, Discover

Not Accepted

  • Cigna
  • Tricare
  • Oscar

We bill using standard procedure and diagnosis codes. If you are unsure about your coverage, please contact our office and we will be happy to verify your benefits.
