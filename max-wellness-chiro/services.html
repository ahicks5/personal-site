Our Approach

Personalized Care for Real Results

At Maximum Wellness, we start by listening. We take the time to understand your lifestyle, your goals, and what is holding you back. Every treatment plan is built around you — delivering effective, evidence-based care without unnecessary costs or cookie-cutter protocols. Our philosophy is simple: find the root cause, treat it properly, and give you the tools to stay well.

Diversified Adjusting

The most widely used chiropractic technique. Uses high-velocity, low-amplitude manual thrusts to restore proper movement and alignment to the spine and joints.

Activator Method

A gentle, low-force approach using a spring-loaded instrument to deliver precise adjustments. Ideal for patients who prefer a softer technique or have sensitive conditions.

Flexion-Distraction Therapy

A gentle, table-based technique that uses rhythmic pumping motions to decompress spinal discs, reduce pressure on nerves, and improve mobility without twisting or thrusting.

Spinal Decompression

Non-surgical decompression therapy that gently stretches the spine to relieve pressure on discs and joints. Effective for herniated discs, sciatica, and chronic back pain.

Soft Tissue / Myofascial Release

Hands-on and instrument-assisted techniques that break up adhesions, reduce muscle tension, and restore flexibility in the muscles, fascia, and connective tissues.

Active Release Technique (ART)

Dr. Tony's specialty. ART is a patented, movement-based soft tissue treatment that targets overused muscles, tendons, ligaments, and nerves to restore full function and relieve pain.

Kinesio Tex Tape

Therapeutic taping applied to muscles and joints to provide support, reduce swelling, improve circulation, and facilitate the body's natural healing process during recovery.

Active Rehab & Physiotherapy

Progressive exercise programs, posture training, and corrective movement strategies designed to strengthen weak areas, prevent re-injury, and keep you performing at your best.

Pediatric Chiropractic

Gentle, age-appropriate techniques for children of all ages. From infants to teens, pediatric adjustments support healthy growth, alignment, and nervous system development.

Pregnancy Chiropractic

Specially adapted care for expecting mothers. Safe, gentle adjustments that help manage back pain, pelvic alignment, and overall comfort throughout every trimester.

Holistic Care

Beyond Adjustments

Nutritional & Lifestyle Counseling

Homeopathic & Naturopathic Remedies

Natural remedy options to complement your chiropractic care. We offer homeopathic and naturopathic solutions for inflammation, stress, immune support, and more.

Sports Injury Rehabilitation

Comprehensive rehab programs for athletes recovering from sports injuries. We combine adjustments, soft tissue work, and progressive exercise to get you back in the game.
