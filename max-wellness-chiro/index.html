Our Core Services

Meet Dr. Tony & Dr. Leslie

A Family Practice Built on Trust

Dr. Tony and Dr. Leslie Maxwell are a husband-wife team, both Doctors of Chiropractic, who founded Maximum Wellness in 2014. They bring complementary expertise to every patient -- Dr. Tony specializes in sports injury and Active Release Techniques, while Dr. Leslie focuses on pediatric and pregnancy care.
