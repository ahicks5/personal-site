Austin Dental Wellness

Full website analysis and ground-up redesign for a biological/holistic dental practice in Austin, TX.

38 Score Before
→
90+ Score After
Web Analysis Visual Design Front-End Dev SEO Accessibility Copywriting

The Problem

Austin Dental Wellness had a website built on the Divi WordPress theme that was actively hurting their business. Page load times ranged from 5 to 8 seconds. The homepage was dominated by outdated COVID-19 protocols instead of their actual services. Navigation links were broken, there were zero testimonials, no calls to action, no analytics tracking, and no structured data for search engines.

For a holistic dental practice trying to attract new patients, their website was doing the opposite — driving people away.

What I Did

Phase 1 — Analysis: I crawled their entire website and scored it 38 out of 100 across design, SEO, content, and technical health. I identified 15 prioritized issues, each explained in business terms: not "your H1 tags are wrong" but "Google can't understand what your practice does, which means patients searching for holistic dentistry in Austin won't find you."

Phase 2 — Report & Pitch: I generated a professional HTML audit report and a polished sales pitch document showing exactly what was wrong, why it mattered to their bottom line, and what the fix would look like.

Phase 3 — Redesign: I built a complete 4-page replacement website from scratch. Hand-coded HTML, CSS, and JavaScript with a nature-forward wellness color palette that reflected their holistic approach. Every page included expanded service content (800+ words per service), FAQ sections, a testimonials module, full SEO foundation with JSON-LD schema, and WCAG AA accessibility compliance.

Key Improvements

  • Load time reduced from 5-8 seconds to under 2 seconds
  • 15 broken navigation links and structural issues fixed
  • 800+ words of original content per service page (up from generic filler)
  • Full JSON-LD structured data for local SEO (DentalClinic schema)
  • WCAG AA accessible — color contrast, ARIA landmarks, keyboard navigation
  • Clear CTAs on every page — "Book a Consultation" prominently placed
  • Overall site score: 38 → projected 90+
