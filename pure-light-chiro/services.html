Our Services

Holistic chiropractic care tailored to your body's unique needs.

Our Approach

Personalized Care for Every Patient

At Pure Light Chiropractic, we believe that no two bodies are the same — and your care shouldn't be either. Dr. Aguilar draws from a wide range of personalized techniques, from traditional manual spinal adjustments to gentle, low-force alternatives, to find the right approach for each patient. Whether you're seeking relief from pain, recovering from an injury, or looking to improve your overall wellness, we meet you where you are and build a plan that fits your body and your goals.

What We Offer

Our Chiropractic Services

Comprehensive care for the whole family — from expecting mothers to young athletes and everyone in between.

Prenatal & Postnatal Care

Safe, gentle adjustments for expecting and new mothers. We help ease pregnancy discomfort, support the body through changes, and promote recovery after birth.

Pediatric Chiropractic

Gentle care for children of all ages. Dr. Aguilar has a special gift for connecting with young patients, making visits comfortable and even fun.

Sports Injury Recovery

Whether you're a weekend warrior or competitive athlete, we help you recover from injuries and get back to performing your best.

Neck & Back Pain Relief

Address the root cause of chronic or acute neck and back pain through targeted spinal adjustments and holistic assessment.

Stress & Wellness Management

Chiropractic care isn't just for pain. Regular adjustments help manage stress, improve sleep, and support your body's overall function.

Holistic Health & Self-Care

We look at the whole picture — physical, mental, and emotional — to help you build sustainable health habits and connect with your body's innate healing ability.
