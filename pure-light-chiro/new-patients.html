Your First Visit

Here's what to expect when you visit Pure Light Chiropractic.

Getting Started

What to Expect

Your first visit is simple, thorough, and designed around you.

Arrive & Check In

Complete your patient paperwork via the online portal beforehand, or arrive 15 minutes early to fill it out in the office.

Consultation & Assessment

Your doctor will perform a thorough consultation and neuro-spinal assessment to understand your unique needs and health history.

Your First Adjustment

Unless there are indications otherwise, you'll receive your first chiropractic adjustment. Your visit will last approximately 30–45 minutes.

Insurance Policy

We do not accept insurance directly. Instead, we provide detailed invoices so you can file claims with your insurance company independently.

This approach allows us to focus fully on your care without third-party negotiations — so every decision is made between you and your doctor.

Our Approach

Techniques

Personalized care that fits your comfort level.

Our staff works with you to find the best approach for your body. We offer everything from traditional manual adjustments to low-force, gentle techniques — and we customize combinations based on your individual needs, comfort level, and health goals. No two patients are the same, and neither are our treatment plans.
