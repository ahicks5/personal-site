Meet Your Providers

Our team is dedicated to connecting you with your body's natural healing ability.

Our mission is to shift people's paradigm of health by connecting them to their body's innate healing ability through empowerment.

Dr. Laine

Founder & Chiropractor
“My passion is to empower people by connecting them to their own inner healer.”

Originally from Louisiana, Dr. Laine followed her passion for holistic health across the country before settling in Austin. She earned her undergraduate degree in Environmental Health from Colorado State University, where she developed a deep appreciation for the connection between our bodies and the natural world.

While in Colorado, she completed her certification as a Massage Therapist and Reiki practitioner, building a hands-on foundation that would shape her approach to healing. She then attended Parker College of Chiropractic in Dallas, where she graduated as class salutatorian — a testament to her dedication and academic excellence.

Today, Dr. Laine practices in Austin and continues to prioritize the same self-care she encourages in her patients. Outside the office, you'll find her connecting with nature, practicing meditation, and embracing motherhood — all of which fuel her ability to show up fully for those she serves.

Dr. Aguilar

Chiropractor, DC, DACNB

Dr. Aguilar brings a diverse professional background and a uniquely compassionate, playful approach to chiropractic care. A certified yoga instructor, he integrates movement and mindfulness into his practice, meeting each patient exactly where they are on their healing journey.

Known for his warmth and humor, Dr. Aguilar has a particular gift for connecting with pediatric patients — making visits feel fun and safe for even the youngest members of the family. While he's quick to bring a smile to the room, his commitment to patient healing is deeply serious and unwavering.

A proud father, Dr. Aguilar understands firsthand the importance of family wellness. He is also the practice's Spanish-speaking provider, ensuring that more members of the Austin community can access quality chiropractic care in the language most comfortable to them. Se habla español.

Krykit

Office Manager

An avid reader and film enthusiast, Krykit grew up right here in Austin. As Office Manager, she engages daily with patients and practitioners alike, continually learning about community wellness and ensuring the office runs seamlessly. Outside of work, you'll find her playing disc golf, adding to her VHS collection, or catching the latest theatrical releases.
