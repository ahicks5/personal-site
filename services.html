Service 01

Web Design & Development

I build complete websites from the ground up. No WordPress themes, no page builders, no bloated frameworks — just clean, hand-coded HTML, CSS, and JavaScript that loads fast, looks sharp, and is built to convert visitors into customers.

Every site is custom. I also audit existing sites, optimize for search engines, and build lead generation tools. Whether you need a new website, a redesign, or to understand why your current one isn't performing — I handle it all.

What's Included

  • Custom website design and hand-coded development
  • Website audits — design, SEO, content, and technical health scoring
  • Mobile-first responsive layouts — looks great on any device
  • Full SEO foundation — meta tags, schema markup, local keywords, sitemap
  • WCAG AA accessibility compliance
  • Conversion-optimized layouts with clear calls to action
  • Lead generation tools and automated outreach systems
  • Full ownership — you get all the code, no lock-in

Who This Is For

Small businesses that need a new website, a redesign, or want to understand why their current site isn't bringing in customers. Healthcare practices, dental offices, wellness providers, professional services — any business that needs a web presence that matches the quality of what they actually do.

Service 02

Excel & Spreadsheet Automation

If your team spends hours every week copying data, formatting reports, or maintaining spreadsheets by hand — I can fix that. I build custom Excel solutions that automate the tedious stuff so your people can focus on work that actually matters.

From one-click macros that replace 30 minutes of manual work to full dashboard systems that pull data from multiple sources — I've saved businesses thousands of man-hours with spreadsheet automation.

What's Included

  • Custom Excel macros and VBA scripts
  • Template design with professional formatting
  • Complex formula optimization and auditing
  • Automated reporting and dashboards
  • Workflow automation that eliminates manual repetitive tasks
  • Data validation and error-proofing for critical spreadsheets

Who This Is For

Businesses drowning in manual spreadsheet work. If your team spends more time formatting cells than making decisions — or if one wrong formula could cost you thousands — you need someone who can build Excel tools that actually work for you, not against you.
