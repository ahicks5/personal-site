Meet the Founder

A Vision for Collaborative Healing

"The body can heal itself. We just have obstruction."

When Charleen Whipple founded The Point Wellness in June 2021, she envisioned a space where practitioners from different healing traditions could work together under one roof. Located in one of Downtown Round Rock's most storied buildings -- the city's first permanent movie theater -- The Point Wellness brings together acupuncture, massage therapy, cryotherapy, and more to help patients find relief and restoration.

With a growing team of 8+ practitioners and a perfect 5.0 Google rating, Charleen's vision of collaborative, patient-centered care has become a cornerstone of the Round Rock wellness community.
