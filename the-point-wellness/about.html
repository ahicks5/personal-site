About The Point Wellness

A team-based approach to healing in downtown Round Rock.

"I wanted to create a space where everybody was on the same team, everybody was co-treating and we could all just take care of one patient on the same platform." — Charleen Whipple, Founder
CW

Charleen Whipple

DOM, MSTCM, L.Ac. — Founder & Lead Acupuncturist

A native of Denver, Colorado, Charleen was raised in an Italian family. She began competitive dance at age 5 and first experienced acupuncture as a teenager for migraines and dance injuries—an introduction that would shape her career.

She earned dual Bachelor of Science degrees in Sports Medicine and Nutrition from Colorado State University, building a strong foundation in how the body moves, heals, and fuels itself.

Charleen graduated from Colorado Chinese Medicine University in 2012 with a Master of Science in Traditional Chinese Medicine, completing 9 trimesters plus clinical rotations.

Nationally licensed under NCCAOM, she practiced in Denver before moving to Austin in 2013, where she worked solo within a chiropractic clinic for 7 years—honing her skills and developing her philosophy of collaborative care.

In June 2021, Charleen opened The Point Wellness to realize her vision of a collaborative, team-based wellness center where practitioners work together under one roof.

Her core belief drives everything the practice does: "The body can heal itself. We just have obstruction." She treats physical, mental, and emotional blockages through acupuncture, cupping, dry needling, and herbal medicine.

Charleen specializes in treating athletes, post-surgery patients, and children with tech neck and anxiety disorders—bringing the same whole-person approach to every patient she sees.

Our Space

A Historic Home for Healing

Nestled in the heart of downtown Round Rock, The Point Wellness occupies a building with deep community roots—the site of Round Rock's first permanent movie theater.

Historic Downtown Location

Located at 116 E Main St in downtown Round Rock, our building was once the city's first permanent movie theater. Today it serves a new purpose—a welcoming space dedicated to your health and well-being.

Calm & Clean Atmosphere

From the moment you walk through our doors, you will feel the difference. Every detail of our space is designed to put you at ease—a calm, clean, spa-inspired environment where healing begins before your session starts.

Collaborative Care Model

What makes The Point Wellness different is how our practitioners work together. Your acupuncturist, massage therapist, and other providers coordinate treatments and cross-refer within the clinic, ensuring every aspect of your care is connected.
