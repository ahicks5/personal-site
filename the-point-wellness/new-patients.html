Getting Started

Your First Visit

We make getting started simple. Here is what to expect at each step of the process.

Book Online

Schedule your appointment through our Vagaro booking system. You will receive an email confirmation with a door code for our front entrance.

Consultation

Your practitioner will discuss your health history, concerns, and goals. Our team-based approach means we can recommend complementary treatments across all our services.

Your Treatment

Relax and let our practitioners work their magic. Sessions range from 30 to 60+ minutes depending on the service. Our calm, clean environment supports your healing journey.
