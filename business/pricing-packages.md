## Overview

Three-tier pricing model designed to capture clients at every budget level. The Starter tier is the entry point and volume driver. Professional is the sweet spot for margin and client satisfaction. Premium is the high-touch offering for clients who want a full-service experience.

---

## Tier 1: Starter — $500-$800

**Best for:** Solopreneurs, new businesses, anyone who needs a professional web presence fast.

**Deliverables:**
- Single landing page (or 1-2 page site)
- Mobile-responsive design, hand-coded HTML/CSS/JS
- Basic on-page SEO (title tags, meta descriptions, alt tags, heading structure)
- Contact form integration
- Google Maps embed (if applicable)
- Social media links
- SSL setup and basic performance optimization
- Lighthouse score target: 90+

**Turnaround:** 5-7 business days

**What's NOT included:**
- Copywriting (client provides content, or add on separately)
- Ongoing maintenance
- Multi-page site architecture
- Monthly SEO work

**When to recommend:** Lead with this for budget-conscious prospects or as a "let me prove myself" offer. Great for converting cold leads from audits. Low barrier to entry, high chance of upsell later.

## Tier 2: Professional — $1,500-$2,500

**Best for:** Established local businesses that need a complete online presence. This is the bread-and-butter tier.

**Deliverables:**

- Mobile-first responsive design, hand-coded
- SEO foundation: keyword research, optimized meta tags, JSON-LD schema markup, XML sitemap, robots.txt

- Contact form with email notification
- Speed optimization (image compression, lazy loading, minification)
- Basic copywriting guidance (headline suggestions, CTA recommendations)
- Lighthouse score target: 90+ across all categories
- 30 days of post-launch support (bug fixes, minor text edits)

**Turnaround:** 2-3 weeks

**What's NOT included:**
- Full copywriting (available as add-on)
- Ongoing monthly SEO
- Blog/CMS functionality
- E-commerce

**When to recommend:** Default recommendation for any established business with 1+ years operating. The case study with Austin Dental Wellness sits at this tier. Use the case study heavily when pitching this package.

## Competitive Positioning

### vs. DIY Website Builders (Wix, Squarespace, WordPress.com)
- **Their pitch:** "Build it yourself for $16-$45/month"
- **Reality:** Bloated code, slow load times, generic templates, no SEO foundation, hours of learning curve, ongoing monthly fees that add up ($192-$540/year, forever)
- **Our pitch:** "For the cost of 2-3 years of Squarespace fees, you get a custom site you own forever, that loads 3-5x faster, and actually ranks on Google. No monthly platform fees. No lock-in."
- **Proof point:** Austin Dental Wellness went from a 38 Lighthouse score on their old site to 90+ on the hand-coded rebuild

### vs. Agencies ($10,000-$50,000+)
- **Their pitch:** "Full-service digital transformation"
- **Reality:** 80% of what they deliver is template-based anyway, 4-8 week timelines, bloated teams, account managers who don't code, revisions take forever
- **Our pitch:** "Agency-quality work at a fraction of the price and timeline. You work directly with the person building your site. No middlemen, no bloated timelines. I deliver in 1-2 weeks what agencies take 2 months to do."

### vs. Other Freelancers (Template-based, $1,000-$5,000)
- **Their pitch:** "Custom WordPress site"
- **Reality:** Most freelancers buy a $60 theme, customize colors and fonts, install 20 plugins that slow the site down, and call it custom. Site breaks when plugins conflict. You need them forever for updates.
- **Our pitch:** "Truly hand-coded — no WordPress, no themes, no plugins. Your site is clean, fast, and you actually own it. Plus I bring a finance background, so every design decision is tied to ROI, not just aesthetics."

## Payment Terms

### Standard Terms (Starter & Professional)
- **50% deposit** due upon signing the service agreement, before work begins
- **50% balance** due upon project completion, before final files are transferred and site goes live
- Payment accepted via Stripe (credit/debit card) or PayPal
- Invoice sent via email with payment link

### Premium Tier Payment Option
- **Option A (standard):** 50% upfront / 50% on delivery
- **Option B (split):** 34% upfront / 33% at midpoint review / 33% on delivery
- Split option available for Premium tier only, at client's request

### Late Payment
- Net 7 on final invoices. If unpaid after 7 days, a friendly reminder is sent.
- If unpaid after 14 days, site will not go live / maintenance pauses until payment is received.
- No late fees for first offense. Repeated late payments may require full upfront payment on future work.

## Revision Policy

### Included Revisions
- **All tiers include 2 rounds of revisions**
- A "round" = one consolidated list of changes submitted by the client
- Revisions cover design tweaks, text changes, layout adjustments, and color/font modifications
- Revisions do NOT cover scope changes (adding new pages, new features, redesigning sections that were already approved)

### Additional Revisions
- Beyond 2 rounds: **$75/hour**, billed in 30-minute increments
- Client is notified before any additional billable work begins
- Scope changes (new pages, new functionality) are quoted separately as add-ons

### How Revisions Work
1. Client reviews staging site and compiles all feedback into one document/email
2. Andrew implements changes within 2-3 business days
3. Client reviews again — this is round 2
4. After round 2 approval, site goes live and final payment is due
5. Any changes after go-live fall under maintenance (if included) or are billed hourly

## Internal Notes

- **Upsell path:** Starter clients are prime candidates for Professional upgrades 3-6 months later. Always plant the seed: "Once you're ready to expand, we can build out a full site."
- **Anchor high:** When on a discovery call, mention the Premium tier first, then work down. Makes Professional feel like a deal.
- **Never discount below Starter minimum ($500).** If someone can't afford $500, they're not the right client. Offer a payment plan before lowering price.
- **Always quote a range, not a fixed number** on the first call. Nail down exact price in the formal proposal after understanding full scope.
- **Track average deal size monthly.** Goal is to push average toward $2,000+ within 8 weeks.
