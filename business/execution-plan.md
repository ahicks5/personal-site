## Overview

This is the week-by-week operational plan for launching and scaling the freelance web design business. The goal: go from zero clients to consistent revenue in 8 weeks, building a sustainable pipeline that generates $6K-$20K/month by the end of the period.

**North star metric:** Revenue closed per week.
**Leading indicator:** Emails sent per week (100 minimum).

## Risk Mitigation

### If Response Rate Is Below 10%

**Diagnosis:** Emails aren't resonating. Could be subject lines, personalization quality, targeting, or timing.

**Actions:**
1. A/B test subject lines — try question-based vs. statement-based vs. curiosity-gap
2. Increase personalization depth — mention specific issues on their actual website, not generic problems
3. Check email deliverability — are emails landing in spam? Send from a warmed-up domain with proper SPF/DKIM
4. Shift targeting — try a different industry vertical or geographic area
5. Change the lead-in — instead of "I noticed issues with your site," try "I built something similar for [case study] — thought you might be interested"
6. Check timing — test sending at different times (early morning vs. lunch vs. evening)

### If No Deals Are Closing (Calls Happen But Proposals Get Rejected)

**Diagnosis:** Pricing too high, proposal not compelling, or not demonstrating enough value on the call.

**Actions:**
1. Review discovery call recordings (if recording) — are you asking enough questions? Are you listening or pitching?
2. Lower entry point — offer a $500 Starter package to get the first client and case study
3. Add a bonus — "Sign this week and I'll include a free month of maintenance"
4. Create urgency — "I only take 3 clients at a time, and I have 1 spot left this month"
5. Offer a money-back guarantee for the first 2-3 clients to eliminate risk
6. Ask rejected prospects why they said no — the feedback is gold

### If You're Overwhelmed (Too Many Projects at Once)

**Diagnosis:** Pipeline is working but capacity is the bottleneck.

**Actions:**
1. Pause outreach and focus on delivering current projects with excellence
2. Prioritize highest-value clients (Professional and Premium tier first)
3. Raise prices immediately — this naturally reduces inbound volume while increasing revenue per client
4. Hire a part-time assistant for outreach, audits, or content creation
5. Create a waitlist — "I'm fully booked for the next 2 weeks. I'll follow up on [date] to get started."
6. Systemize further — invest time in templates and reusable components that cut build time

### If a Client Project Goes Badly

**Diagnosis:** Scope creep, miscommunication, or client is difficult.

**Actions:**
1. Refer to the signed service agreement — scope, revisions, and timeline are all documented
2. Have an honest conversation — "Here's what was agreed, here's where we are, here's how we get back on track"
3. If the client is unreasonable, offer a partial refund and part ways professionally
4. Document what went wrong and update processes to prevent it next time
5. Never let one bad client derail the entire business — keep outreach and pipeline moving regardless

### If Social Media Isn't Generating Leads

**Diagnosis:** Content isn't converting to business. Common in the first 4-6 weeks.

**Actions:**
1. Don't panic — social media is a long game. Direct outreach (cold email) is the primary revenue driver in the first 8 weeks.
2. Double down on audit teardown content — it consistently performs well and demonstrates expertise
3. Engage more — comment on others' posts, reply to DMs, participate in conversations. Posting without engaging is shouting into the void.
4. Add clearer CTAs — end every post with "DM me for a free audit" or "Link in bio"
5. Try video content — Loom walkthroughs of audits, short-form build clips
6. Track what gets engagement vs. what doesn't. Do more of what works, cut what doesn't.

## Internal Notes

- **Week 1 is about momentum, not perfection.** Don't spend 3 days perfecting an email template. Send 20 emails that are 80% good instead of 5 emails that are 100% good. You'll learn more from sending than from polishing.
- **Revenue in Week 1-2 is $0 and that's fine.** The pipeline needs time to build. Don't get discouraged. The work you do in Week 1-2 produces revenue in Week 3-4.
- **Track everything from Day 1.** The spreadsheet is your source of truth. Fill it in every single day. The data will tell you what to optimize in Week 3+.
- **Protect client delivery time.** Once you have paying clients, their work comes first. Outreach and social media happen in the margins. Never miss a client deadline because you were sending cold emails.
- **Celebrate small wins.** First response to a cold email. First audit requested. First discovery call. First proposal sent. First deposit received. These are all milestones worth acknowledging.
- **Review this plan every Friday.** Compare actual numbers to targets. Adjust the next week's plan based on what the data tells you. This document is a living plan, not a rigid script.
