Our Story

More Than a Spa — A Sanctuary

Moonlight Head Spa was born from a deep belief that true beauty starts at the crown. Drawing from Japanese and Vietnamese healing traditions, every treatment is designed to restore not just your scalp and hair, but your sense of inner calm.

Tucked inside Renu Well Aesthetics in North Austin, our intimate studio is a space where time slows down. Expect sound healing bowls, aromatherapy, warm stones, and intuitive hands that know exactly where you hold your tension.
