About Me

The Person Behind the Pixels

A finance mind with a builder's hands — and a genuine obsession with making technology work for people.

Professional photo coming soon

Hey, I'm Andrew.

I didn't take the traditional path into web development. I graduated from the University of Notre Dame with a degree in finance — which means I think about websites the way I think about any business asset: what's the return?

That mindset is what sets me apart. I don't just make things look good (though they do). I build solutions that serve a purpose — whether that's a website that attracts customers, a spreadsheet that saves your team hours of manual work, or a data model that tells you what's going to happen next.

I spent years automating business processes — building Excel macros and VBA scripts that saved thousands of hours of manual work across multiple departments. That led me into Python automation and data science — web scraping, machine learning models, and sports analytics that turn raw data into competitive advantages.

Now I combine all three skill sets to help businesses work smarter. No unnecessary complexity. No bloated frameworks. Just clean, effective technology that does what it's supposed to do — whether that's a website, a spreadsheet system, or a data pipeline.

I'm based in Austin, TX, and I work primarily with small businesses in healthcare, dental, wellness, and professional services — the kinds of businesses where a great website directly translates to more appointments, more calls, and more revenue.
